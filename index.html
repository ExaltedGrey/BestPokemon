SNES Field Guide

  • Swap Lead (Tab / Q / E): Rotate which Pokémon leads your party line to use its overworld Field Skill!
  • Field Skills (C / Space):
    • 🔥 Ember Torch (Fire/Dragon): Light dark shrine braziers & melt frost/shadow blocks.
    • 🌿 Razor Cut (Grass/Bug): Slice overgrown shadow vines & forage berry bushes.
    • 🪨 Tremor Dig (Ground/Rock): Smash cracked boulders & unearth buried relics.
    • ⚡ Surge / Angler (Water/Electric): Power ancient runes & fish at Willow Pier!
  • Dragon Resonance: Awaken the Ancient Dragon Statue in Verdant Wilds to transform your starter in battle like Breath of Fire!
  • Day & Night Cycle: Different wild Pokémon appear at Dusk/Night (Gastly, Zubat, Houndour).
